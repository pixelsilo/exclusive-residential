This policy explains how Exclusive Construction Group collects and uses personal information through this website, and the rights you have. We keep it simple: we only use your information to reply to you and to understand how the website is used.

Who we are

This website is run by Exclusive Residential Ltd, trading as Exclusive Construction Group, a company registered in England (company number 09421240) at 288 Bishopsgate, London EC2M 4QP. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email contact@excl.group, call 020 7501 1660, or write to us at the address above.

What we collect and why

When you contact us

If you send us an enquiry through our contact form, we receive your name, company, email address, phone number and message. We use this only to reply to your enquiry and, if you want to work with us, to discuss your project.

Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us and, where your enquiry relates to a potential project, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We do not use your details for marketing and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Website analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed and how people find us. This involves cookies and information about your device, browser and approximate location (derived from your IP address). We do not use this to identify you or for advertising.

Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can give or withdraw at any time using Cookie settings. Analytics does not run unless you allow it.

Maps

Our Contact page can show a Google Map. With your consent, the map loads from Google, which receives your IP address and device information when it does. If you don't consent, the map stays switched off. Our lawful basis is your consent.

Running the website securely

Like most websites, our hosting and security services process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, so that the site can be delivered, protected from spam and abuse, and kept working. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information or share it for marketing. We use a small number of trusted service providers who process information on our behalf and only on our instructions:

  • Webflow: hosts this website and receives contact form submissions.
  • Google: Google Analytics (only with your consent) and Google Maps (only with your consent).
  • Cloudflare: security and spam protection for the website and contact form.
  • jsDelivr: delivers parts of the website's code, which means it receives your IP address.
  • Our email provider: contact form enquiries are sent to our inbox.
  • Our website developer: maintains this website and may access form submissions to provide support.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

International transfers

Some of these providers, including Webflow and Google, are based in or process data in the United States. Where your information is transferred outside the UK, it is protected by recognised safeguards, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: up to two years after our last contact with you, unless you become a client, in which case we keep project records for as long as needed for the project and our legal, tax and insurance obligations (usually up to six years after it ends).
  • Google Analytics data: up to 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically.
  • Your cookie choice: 12 months, after which we ask again.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) and, where it applies, the EU GDPR, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to us using it on the basis of our legitimate interests;
  • ask us to transfer information you gave us to another organisation;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (for analytics and maps, use Cookie settings), without affecting anything done before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, email contact@excl.group. We will reply within one month. There is usually no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.
