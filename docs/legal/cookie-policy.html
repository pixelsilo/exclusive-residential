This policy explains how Exclusive Construction Group uses cookies and similar technologies on this website, and how you can control them.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Some are essential for a site to work. Others, such as analytics cookies, are optional and we only use them if you agree.

Your choices

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept or reject optional cookies. Nothing optional runs until you choose, and rejecting is as easy as accepting. You can change your mind at any time using Cookie settings, also linked at the bottom of every page.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, parts of the site may not work properly.

Cookies we use

Essential (always on)

  • ecg_consent (Exclusive Construction Group): remembers your cookie choices. Expires after 12 months.
  • __cf_bm and _cfuvid (Cloudflare): protect the website and contact form from bots and abuse. __cf_bm expires after 30 minutes; _cfuvid is deleted when you close your browser.

Analytics (only with your consent)

  • _ga (Google Analytics): distinguishes visitors so we can count visits. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_KC3QGD9MKE (Google Analytics): keeps track of your current visit. Expires after 2 years.

Google Analytics tells us which pages people visit and how they find the site. We don't use it for advertising, and the data it collects is deleted after 14 months. If you withdraw consent, these cookies are deleted.

Maps (only with your consent)

The map on our Contact page is provided by Google Maps. It doesn't set cookies on our site, but when it loads, Google receives your IP address and device information. It only loads if you allow Maps, or if you choose to load it on the Contact page.

Other services that receive your IP address

To deliver the website, our hosting provider (Webflow), security provider (Cloudflare) and code delivery network (jsDelivr) receive your IP address when you visit. They don't set optional cookies.

More information

For how we handle personal information more generally, and your rights, see our Privacy Policy. For independent guidance on cookies, visit the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
